A framework-oriented engine project focused on maintainable architecture and rendering flexibility across APIs. Odyssean is the middle step in my engine lineage: it is where I first worked across both OpenGL and Vulkan backends and shaped systems as replaceable modules, and the lessons from that experimentation - the GPU abstraction boundary, the modular integration patterns - carried directly into AetherCore, the engine that superseded it.
